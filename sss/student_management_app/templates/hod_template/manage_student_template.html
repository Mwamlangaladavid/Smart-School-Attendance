Face Recognition Guide

How to enroll students for face recognition:
  1. Click the camera icon () next to a student to capture their face
  2. Alternatively, open student details and use the "Face Recognition" tab to upload a clear face image
  3. For best results, ensure good lighting and a clear view of the face
  4. After enrolling students, click "Train Model" to update the recognition system
Tips for better recognition:
  • Capture multiple angles of the student's face
  • Ensure adequate lighting during enrollment
  • The student should look directly at the camera
  • Remove glasses or other face coverings during enrollment
Students must be enrolled in face recognition before they can be identified for attendance.
